Frequently Asked Questions

What does BrandLens AI check?

Tone, banned/preferred terminology, factual claims against approved sources, and accessibility rules.

Does AI rewrite or publish automatically?

No. AI only suggests fixes with citations; authorised humans accept edits and approve publication.

What if a claim has no approved source?

BrandLens AI flags it distinctly as UNSUPPORTED instead of guessing whether it is true or false.

Can multiple teammates share one workspace?

Yes. Admins invite teammates by email with Creator, Reviewer, Manager, or Admin permissions.
