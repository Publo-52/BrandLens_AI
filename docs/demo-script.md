# BrandLens AI — 5-Minute Demonstration Script

| Time | Demonstration Segment | Target Narrative & Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:30** | **Problem & Purpose** | Explain pre-publication brand compliance challenges: fragmented guidelines, slow reviews, hallucinated facts, and unexplainable approvals. Introduce BrandLens AI as an explainable governance platform where humans stay in control. |
| **0:30–1:10** | **Brand Knowledge Base** | Open fictional organisation workspace (*NovaTech*). Demonstrate versioned guidelines, approved facts library, deterministic terminology rules, and color palette tokens. |
| **1:10–1:50** | **Content Intake** | Creator submits draft social media copy / document with intentional violations (banned phrase, exaggerated claim, unapproved product name). |
| **1:50–2:50** | **Findings & Citations** | Review findings generated: deterministic terminology flag with 100% confidence, tone mismatch with cited voice guideline, and an **Unsupported Claim** clearly marked because no approved fact exists. |
| **2:50–3:35** | **Intent-Preserving Suggestions & Diff** | Creator reviews suggested corrections: accepts one fix, edits another, rejects one with a reason. Show version comparison with side-by-side diff. |
| **3:35–4:20** | **Human Review & Approval** | Manager/Reviewer inspects evidence, leaves a comment, overrides a low-confidence flag with an audit-logged reason, and grants formal approval. |
| **4:20–4:50** | **Governance Insights & Audit Trail** | Show immutable audit trail capturing every decision. Display governance dashboard showing recurring errors and guideline ambiguity candidates. |
| **4:50–5:00** | **Disclosure & Next Steps** | Disclose AI model used, deterministic vs AI checks breakdown, and conclude. |
